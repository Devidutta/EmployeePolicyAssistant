## Leave Policy
Employees may apply for planned leave in advance through the designated leave system. Approval depends on project commitments and manager confirmation. Emergency leave should be reported to the manager as soon as possible, followed by a formal request in the leave system once the employee is able to do so.

## Work From Home Policy
Eligible employees may work remotely based on project requirements and manager approval. Team availability and security requirements must be followed, including the use of company-approved devices and secure network connections. Employees on client-facing projects may need to follow additional client site policies.

## Learning Policy
Employees are encouraged to complete role-relevant learning programs and maintain their learning progress. Learning hours may be tracked as part of periodic performance reviews. Managers may recommend specific courses based on project needs and career growth plans.

## Certification Policy
Role-relevant professional certifications may be supported subject to applicable approval and reimbursement rules. Employees should discuss certification plans with their manager before enrolling, and retain proof of completion for reimbursement processing.

## Travel Policy
Business travel should follow the required approval process and applicable travel guidelines. Travel requests must be submitted in advance with project and cost center details. Reimbursement for approved expenses follows the standard expense policy and requires original receipts.

## Timesheet Policy
Employees must submit timesheets within the defined reporting cycle and ensure project codes and effort details are accurate. Late or incomplete timesheets may delay project billing and payroll processing. Corrections to submitted timesheets should be routed through the manager for approval.
